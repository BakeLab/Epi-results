# Tuza’s conjecture for a larger split-graph class

Finite simple split graphs with a specified partition V = C ⊔ I, where C induces K₈ and I is independent. Among vertices of I with at least two neighbors in C, at most three distinct neighborhoods occur. Each type may have arbitrary multiplicity.

**τ△(G) ≤ 2ν△(G).**

| Published scope | Épi |
| :-- | :-- |
| At most two active neighborhood types | At most three active neighborhood types |

Here τ△ is the minimum number of edges meeting every triangle, and ν△ is the maximum number of edge-disjoint triangles. The graph may have arbitrarily many vertices; eight is the clique-part size.

The reduction covers all 872 three-type classes. Known arguments cover 35 nested classes and 277 further classes. Exact triangle covers and edge-disjoint triangle packings verify the remaining 560 classes, using 18,444 boxes. A truncation argument extends the finite check to arbitrary multiplicities.
