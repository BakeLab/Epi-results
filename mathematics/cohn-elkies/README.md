# Cohn–Elkies linear-programming bounds

| Quantity | Published comparison | Épi |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Cohn–Elkies LP, d = 9 | 1/20 | **0.058044329039** |
| Cohn–Elkies LP, d = 10 | 1/24 | **0.056433853144** |
| Cohn–Elkies LP, d = 11 | 1/24 | **0.057731808202** |
| Cohn–Elkies LP, d = 13 | 1/28 | **0.066440082613** |

These are lower bounds on the optimal Cohn–Elkies linear-programming output, in center-density normalization. The exact support and discrete Fourier inequalities were checked by a separate implementation.

In dimensions 10 and 11, the bounds exceed the known upper bounds on actual sphere-packing density. The Cohn–Elkies linear program therefore cannot give the exact density in either dimension.
